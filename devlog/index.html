Devlog

Every milestone of Altarhold, newest first. Each post ends with a hash of the game's private source at that point, so the dates can be checked. The timeline lists when each mechanic was built.

  1. #0102026-11-04

    Find Match, ranked and ten new faces

    Matchmaking, a player dashboard with friends and parties, a ranked ladder, a Blender-made world and ten heroes on the way.

  2. #0092026-11-03

    A name, new looks and twelve relics

    The game becomes Altarhold, eight heroes rebuilt in 3D, six new relics, accounts and ratings, and a launcher.

  3. #0082026-10-31

    Kills, a bronze HUD and new heroes

    Rewarding hero kills, a carved bronze HUD, a new hero select, starting attributes, and three more redrawn heroes.

  4. #0072026-10-31

    Going pixel art

    A new 32-bit-style look: sprite heroes in eight directions and a painted pixel map.

  5. #0062026-10-31

    Sound, feel and the first alpha build

    Sound for every hit, springier hero motion, a cheaper 5v5 server, and the first tester build.

  6. #0052026-10-29

    The stickman look

    All ten heroes as stickmen, a cartoon forest map, and an effect for every attack and skill.

  7. #0042026-10-24

    First heroes in full art

    Orin Ash and Kira Vex get real models, animations and ability effects.

  8. #0032026-10-24

    Online play and relic arenas

    Dedicated servers, walled relic arenas in the jungle, ten heroes, a real map, and the first hero model.

  9. #0022026-10-23

    Six heroes and fog of war

    Hero select, five new heroes, and fog of war without wards.

  10. #0012026-10-22

    A MOBA with no shop

    The idea behind the game, its two hard rules, and the first playable prototype.
