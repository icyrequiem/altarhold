Devlog #11: A new map, skins and a hero library

5 November 2026 · Ynairally

This update is mostly about the battlefield. The map was rebuilt from the ground up, heroes can now wear skins, and the dashboard got a library where you can read up on every hero. All of it, along with the ranked ladder and hero select from devlog #10, is in alpha 0.8.0.

A new map

The first map borrowed its shape from the classic three-lane layout. The new one keeps three lanes and a river but is our own design. The river winds from corner to corner. Lane corners are rounded. The jungle is built from groves and trails instead of blocky walls, and both relic arenas now sit in open ground, so fights there have room to move.

The jungle has grown to twenty-two camps, eleven on each side and mirrored, with more golems, wolves, toads and raptors to hunt for experience.

High ground

Each base, from its core out to its last ring of towers, now sits on high ground. From the low ground you can't see up onto it unless one of your units is already there, and basic attacks fired up at it can miss. Abilities always land. Pushing into a base now takes vision and commitment, and defenders get a real edge at home.

Skins

Heroes can now wear skins. Orin Ash gets the first two: Off-Duty Orin, in a red shirt and jeans, and Ashguard Orin, in plate armour and a cape. Like the heroes, both are modelled in 3D and baked into pixel art in all eight directions. You pick a skin you own with the arrows under the altar in hero select, and everyone in the match sees it. Skins are looks only, so they never change how a hero plays.

A hero library

The dashboard has a new Hero Library. It lists every hero by attribute with role tabs and search. Pick one to see them on the altar with their starting attributes, stats, growth per level, full kit and every skin, showing which you own. The ten heroes who are modelled but still waiting for their kits are listed there too, marked Coming Soon.
