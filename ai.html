How Altarhold uses AI

Altarhold is made by a solo developer, and some of its art and assets were made with the help of AI tools. Last updated 11 November 2026.

Art and assets

AI tools were used during development to help create some of the game's visual art, such as concept art and hero portraits, and some of its sound effects. Every asset is chosen, reviewed and edited by the developer before it ships.

Most of the game's visuals, including the hero, minion and map sprites and the ability effects, are rendered from 3D models built for Altarhold. Most sounds and all of the music are synthesised for the game.

While you play

  • Nothing in the game is generated by AI while you play. There is no AI chat, AI voice or AI-made content in a match.
  • Your account data and matches are not used to train AI.
