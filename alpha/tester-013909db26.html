Getting started

  1. Run AltarholdSetup.exe. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the alpha isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  2. The installer needs no admin rights. It puts Altarhold on your Desktop and in the Start menu, then opens the launcher.
  3. The launcher downloads the game, keeps it up to date and shows the patch notes. Press Play when it's ready. Next time, start it from the Altarhold shortcut.
  4. To remove the game, use Settings > Apps or Uninstall Altarhold in the Start menu.
  5. In the game, sign in with your Altarhold account. No account yet? Register here first. Then press Find Match.

Report bugs and feedback in our Discord.

Controls

Right-click to move and attack, A to attack-move, Q W E R to aim an ability and left-click to cast, Ctrl+Q/W/E/R to learn one, B to recall, Tab for the scoreboard, Space to centre the camera.
