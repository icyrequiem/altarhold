Getting started

  1. Unzip the launcher somewhere you can write to, such as your Desktop or Documents (not Program Files), then double-click AltarholdLauncher.exe.
  2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the alpha isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. The launcher downloads the game, keeps it up to date and shows the patch notes. Press Play when it's ready.
  4. In the game, press Play online, pick a hero and press Ready. Bots fill empty seats.

Controls

Right-click to move and attack, A to attack-move, Q W E R to aim an ability and left-click to cast, Ctrl+Q/W/E/R to learn one, B to recall, Tab for the scoreboard, Space to centre the camera.
