Privacy policy

Altarhold is a 5v5 online battle game made by Ynairally. This page explains what we keep about you when you make an account and play, why, and how to delete it. Last updated 8 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Your account: email address, password (stored only as a hash by our sign-in provider), username, display name, country, and that you confirmed you are 13 or older. If you register on this website we also keep your birthday, privately, to check your age, and whether you asked to join the closed alpha.
  • Your profile and games: your rank and rating, wins and losses, match history (heroes, kills, deaths, assists, level, relics, result), friends and friend requests, party invites, and the hero skins you own.
  • While you play online: the game server sees your IP address to send you the match, and records every match as a replay (the moves and abilities of each hero) to check fair play and balance the game.
  • On your device: the game saves your sign-in (a renewal token) and settings on your own device.

We do not show ads, sell your data or use analytics or tracking tools. The game has no in-app purchases.

What is public

Your username, display name, country, rank and match results can be seen by other players and on leaderboards. Your email, birthday and IP address are never shown to anyone.

Why we use it

To run your account and sign you in, to match you with players of similar skill, to show your stats, friends and history, to keep games fair (cheat and abuse checks), and to email you about your account or an alpha invite you asked for.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores accounts and stats and handles sign-in.
  • Resend sends account emails (confirmation and password reset).
  • Google Cloud hosts the game servers.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website; pages load fonts from Google Fonts.

These services may store data outside your country. They process it only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. Server logs and replays are kept only as long as we need them to check fair play and balance the game.

Delete your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the game: sign in, then press Delete account at the bottom of the player dashboard and confirm.
  • Without the game: email altarhold2026@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We do it within 30 days.

Deleting removes your account, email, profile, rank, friends and skins for good. Your past match results stay in other players' match history, without any link to you and named "Deleted player".

Children

Altarhold is for players 13 and older. We don't knowingly keep accounts of younger children; if you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights and contact

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or ask any question about this policy, at altarhold2026@gmail.com. If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
